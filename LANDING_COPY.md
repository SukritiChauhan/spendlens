## FAQ

**Q: Is SpendLens really free?**
A: Yes, completely free. No credit card, no login required. We show you results instantly and only ask for your email after you have seen your savings.

**Q: How do you know the pricing data is accurate?**
A: We pull pricing directly from each vendor's official pricing page and update it weekly. Every number in our audit engine traces to a source URL with a verification date.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: We do not store your company name or email until you choose to share it. The public shareable URL strips all identifying information and only shows tools and savings numbers.

**Q: Why is this free? What is the catch?**
A: SpendLens is built by Credex, which sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. If your audit shows significant savings, we will let you know that Credex can help you save even more. You are never obligated to buy anything.

**Q: What AI tools do you support?**
A: We support Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. We update the list regularly as new tools launch.
