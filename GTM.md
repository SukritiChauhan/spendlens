# Go To Market Strategy

## Exact Target User

The primary target is an **Engineering Manager at a Series A startup** (15-50 employees) who manages a team of 5-15 engineers. They approved AI tool subscriptions 6-12 months ago when the team was smaller, never revisited the decision, and now have 3-5 overlapping subscriptions nobody fully uses. They are not the CFO but they feel the pressure when the CFO asks "why are we spending $4,000/month on AI tools?"

Secondary target: **Indie hackers and solo founders** running side projects who pay for ChatGPT Plus and Cursor Pro but could get by with cheaper alternatives.

## How to get first 100 users in 30 days with zero budget

**Week 1 — Hacker News launch:**
Post "Show HN: I built a free tool to audit your AI tool spend" with a genuine story about how startups overpay. HN drives 500-2000 visits on a good Show HN. Target: 30-50 audits completed.

**Week 2 — Reddit posts:**
Post in r/ExperiencedDevs: "I analysed 50 startup AI tool stacks — here is what I found" with real anonymised data from early users. Link to SpendLens naturally. Target: 20-30 new users.

**Week 3 — Twitter thread:**
Write a thread: "Most startups are wasting $X/month on AI tools. Here is a breakdown of the 5 most common mistakes." End with SpendLens link. Tag relevant accounts. Target: 20-30 new users.

**Week 4 — Direct outreach:**
DM 50 Engineering Managers on LinkedIn with: "Hey, I built a free tool that audits AI tool spend. Took me 2 minutes to find $300/month in savings for my own stack. Want to try it?" Target: 20-30 new users.
