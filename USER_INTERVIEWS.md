# User Interviews — SpendLens

## Interview 1

### Name / Initials
Sriram Pandey

### Role & Company Stage
Data Analyst at SHFTX (Growing Startup)

### Direct Quotes
> “Most AI tools are useful, but companies end up paying for features they barely use.”

> “For data-related workflows, API costs can quietly scale very fast.”

> “I compare tools based on workflow efficiency, not just subscription price.”

> “An AI audit platform would help if it clearly showed unnecessary spending.”

### Most Surprising Thing They Said
Many businesses continue paying for AI tools simply because they are integrated into workflows, even when usage drops significantly.

### What It Changed About Our Design
We added ideas like inactive subscription alerts, workflow-based analytics, monthly usage reports, and AI spend optimization recommendations for teams.

## Interview 2

### Name / Initials
Divya Kumari

### Role & Company Stage
Data Architecture at Alankara Vasana (Early-stage Business)

### Direct Quotes
> “Small businesses usually subscribe to tools without properly tracking usage.”

> “If AI tools save time and improve workflow, people are willing to pay more.”

> “Most users don’t even realize how much they spend monthly across different subscriptions.”

> “A dashboard showing all AI expenses in one place would actually be useful.”

### Most Surprising Thing They Said
She mentioned that convenience matters more than pricing for many businesses once a tool becomes part of daily operations.

### What It Changed About Our Design
We decided to focus more on centralized expense tracking and easy-to-read dashboards so businesses can quickly identify unnecessary subscriptions and hidden recurring costs.

## Interview 3

### Name / Initials
Nitin Kumar

### Role & Company Stage
BOA at SHFTX (Growing Startup)

### Direct Quotes
> “Paid AI tools are good, but most people don’t use even half the features they pay for.”

> “Companies should track AI expenses the same way they track cloud costs.”

> “A tool that gives spending insights and usage reports would save a lot of money.”

> “People care about performance first, but pricing becomes important at scale.”

### Most Surprising Thing They Said
He compared AI subscription management to cloud infrastructure optimization, showing how AI costs may become a major operational expense in the future.

### What It Changed About Our Design
This pushed us to think beyond subscriptions and include analytics features like usage efficiency reports, scaling insights, and AI ROI tracking inside SpendLens.
