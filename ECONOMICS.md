# Unit Economics

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. A typical deal:
- Company spends $2,000/month on AI tools
- Credex offers 25% discount = $500/month savings for the customer
- Credex margin on the deal = approximately 10-15% of face value
- Annual contract value to Credex = $2,000 x 12 x 12.5% margin = $3,000/year per customer

Conservative estimate: **$1,500 LTV per converted customer** (assuming 6 month average retention)
Optimistic estimate: **$5,000 LTV per converted customer** (12 month retention, larger deal)

## Customer Acquisition Cost at each channel

| Channel | Effort | Estimated CAC |
|---|---|---|
| Show HN post | 2 hours writing | $0 — time only |
| Reddit posts | 1 hour per post | $0 — time only |
| Twitter thread | 2 hours writing | $0 — time only |
| LinkedIn DMs | 3 hours per 50 DMs | $0 — time only |
| Credex existing customers | 30 min email | $0 — already have relationship |

At zero paid budget, CAC is effectively $0 in cash. At $50/hour opportunity cost:
- Show HN: $100 CAC if it drives 50 audits and 5 leads (2 hours / 5 leads x $50)
- LinkedIn DMs: $150 CAC if 50 DMs drive 3 leads (3 hours / 3 leads x $50)

## Conversion funnel math

Assumptions based on similar B2B lead gen tools:

- 1,000 visitors/month
- 20% complete an audit = 200 audits
- 30% of auditors enter email = 60 leads
- 15% of leads book a Credex consultation = 9 consultations
- 40% of consultations convert to credit purchase = 3-4 customers/month

At $1,500 LTV per customer:
**3 customers x $1,500 = $4,500/month revenue from 1,000 visitors**

Break-even CAC = $4,500 / 60 leads = $75 per lead

## What has to be true for $1M ARR in 18 months

$1M ARR = $83,333/month revenue
At $1,500 LTV and 6 month payback: need ~55 new customers/month
At 40% close rate from consultations: need ~138 consultations/month
At 15% consultation rate from leads: need ~920 leads/month
At 30% email capture rate: need ~3,067 audits/month
At 20% audit completion rate: need ~15,000 visitors/month

**What has to be true:**
1. SpendLens gets featured on 2-3 major newsletters (TLDR, Hacker Newsletter)
2. Credex closes its existing pipeline of 50+ warm leads using SpendLens as a free tool
3. The shareable URL feature drives viral loops — each user shares with 0.3 friends on average
4. Credex runs paid LinkedIn ads targeting Engineering Managers at Series A-B startups at $50 CPL
5. Average deal size grows to $3,000 LTV as Credex moves upmarket
